# Regional art: staff review sheet

**For:** AblePoint regional staff (Central Coast, Newcastle, Dubbo, Orange, Tamworth, Gunnedah) and the compliance owner.
**What:** six small illustrations (`regional-art-for-staff-review.png`, same folder) used at the top of each region card, plus licensed photographs (credited on the Image credits page) on each region page.
**Status:** DRAFT. Not approved for the public site.

## Please check each one

| Region | What is shown | Questions |
|---|---|---|
| Central Coast | Coast, headland, waves, beach, gulls | Does it feel like our coast? Anything that misrepresents the area? |
| Newcastle | Harbour, lighthouse on a headland, ship | Is the lighthouse a fair, respectful cue? Anything people in Newcastle would find off? |
| Dubbo | Open plains, one gum tree, striped fields, big sun | Is "plains and sky" the right feeling? |
| Orange | Orchard rows with orange fruit, rolling hills | Is orchards a fair cue for Orange today? |
| Tamworth | Hills at dusk with a guitar | Is the guitar tasteful and appropriate, or a cliché to drop? |
| Gunnedah | Hills, gum tree, a small koala silhouette | Is the koala appropriate, or a cliché to drop? |

## Also check

1. Nothing in the art is an Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander motif (none is used on purpose). Is there any local cultural cue we should consult on, with the right people, before adding anything?
2. The photographs are freely licensed placeholders (landscapes, no identifiable faces). Are they the right places? Do you have better photos you can supply with permission?
3. Anything missing or wrong about how your region is described in words (the draft banner on each region page lists what we still need from you).

## How to give feedback

Mark each row **Keep / Change / Drop** and add a short reason. Send to the project lead. Nothing is published until the compliance owner approves.
