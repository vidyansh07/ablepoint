Verdict

Yes, you can do this, and the first wins are mechanical. The current site has template placeholders in its footer on every page, a referral form that collects NDIS numbers with no privacy statement, two H1s on key pages, no structured data, location pages with about 300 words of generic copy, and a SIL page with no vacancies, photos or funding explanation. None of that needs talent; it needs an afternoon and a checklist. The hard part starts after: proving cost per qualified enquiry with real numbers.

The order matters more than the tools. Measurement goes in before content, content before ads, ads before automation that scales them. The rebuild runs beside the content work, not ahead of it, because the content is the actual constraint.

What the owners should hear from you in week one: three numbers you will report every Friday (qualified enquiries, cost per enquiry, time to first response), one compliance fix you made the same day (the "approved provider" wording), and a date for the new site.

Ad budget: what each level buys

Modelled from Australian NDIS benchmarks: clicks at A$3.50–8 (regional NSW sits low in that range), 6–10% of clicks becoming enquiries on a good landing page, 25–45% of enquiries becoming participants. Agencies report A$40–90 per qualified enquiry as healthy and A$150–250 as what badly structured accounts pay. Treat these as the range to beat, not a promise; replace every number with your own after 30 days of data.

Monthly spendSplitClicksEnquiriesCPLParticipantsWhat it is good for
A$1,000Google Search only, one region125–2858–28A$36–1252–12Proving the funnel in the region with the most capacity. Below 30 conversions a month, so manual bidding, not Target CPA.
A$1,500Google Search, two regions190–43011–43A$35–1353–19The level most providers start at. Enough data to compare regions.
A$2,500A$1,800 Google · A$700 Meta vacancies450–90025–75A$33–1007–33Google across regions plus a real Meta vacancy test. Target CPA becomes usable on the main campaign.
A$3,500A$2,300 Google · A$700 Meta vacancies · A$500 recruitment650–1,25035–100A$35–1009–45Only when intake can absorb it. Recruitment spend protects delivery capacity.

Clicks = spend ÷ CPC range (Meta clicks at A$1.50–3.50). Enquiries = clicks × 6–10% (Meta leads at a lower 15–30% participant rate). Participant value under NDIS price limits is usually many thousands of dollars a year, which is why even a A$125 enquiry can be sound. Agencies charge A$1,500–5,000 a month to manage this; you are replacing that fee.

Recommendation to put in front of the owners: approve A$1,500 a month for 60 days starting week 3, in the region with capacity, with a stop rule (pause if cost per qualified enquiry is above A$150 after 30 days) and a scale rule (move to A$2,500 if it is under A$90).
