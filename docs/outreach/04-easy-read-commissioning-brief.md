# Easy Read commissioning brief (DRAFT)

**Purpose.** Produce and test Easy Read versions of AblePoint's key information so people with intellectual disability can use our services independently.

**Pages (first batch).** About AblePoint; how to talk to us / make a referral; what happens after you contact us; privacy (what we collect and why); feedback and complaints; accessibility.

**Requirements.**
1. Written by an Easy Read specialist, following the Australian Government Style Manual's Easy Read guidance (stylemanual.gov.au) and Inclusion Australia's "A Guide to Commissioning Easy Read Resources".
2. Pictures made for the text (commissioned artwork or a properly licensed picture library; **not** a non-commercial symbol set). We will not use Easy Read pictures unless their licence allows commercial use.
3. **Tested with people with intellectual disability**, with a short report of what changed after testing. Participants paid fairly.
4. Delivered as accessible web content (real text, not images of text; alt text; large type; clear layout) and as a printable PDF.
5. Compliance owner signs off the final wording before publication.

**Deliverables.** Final Easy Read text and pictures, source files, test report, licence for reuse and modification.
