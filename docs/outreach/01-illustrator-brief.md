# Illustrator brief: AblePoint Australia (DRAFT for owner approval)

**About us.** AblePoint Australia is an NDIS provider based in Gosford, NSW, supporting people with disability on the Central Coast, in Newcastle, Dubbo, Orange, Tamworth and Gunnedah.

**What we need.** A small illustration system we can own and reuse across our website, forms and print:
1. A hero illustration for the home page (warm, everyday, welcoming).
2. Four spot scenes: services, vacancies (accessible homes), guides, referral/contact.
3. 8 to 12 simple pictures for Easy Read pages (people doing everyday things: calling, filling in a form, visiting a home, meeting a support worker).
4. Optional: regional vignettes for six NSW regions (we have placeholders drawn in-house).

**Style.** Friendly, calm, premium, flat or lightly textured. Use our palette: steel blue, olive green, plum, warm orange (hex values supplied). People should be shown with dignity and agency, in everyday moments, on equal footing with others. No pity, no "inspiration" framing, no medical clichés. A range of abilities, including less visible ones, and a range of ages and backgrounds. We welcome the artist's own perspective and lived experience.

**Deliverables.** Layered SVG (preferred) plus PNG at 2x; a style sheet; alt-text suggestions are not needed (the art is decorative).

**Licence.** Perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive or exclusive (please quote both) for commercial web and print use by AblePoint, including modification and use in Easy Read documents. Credit the artist wherever they wish; we are glad to credit and to link to their work.

**Accessibility.** Art is decorative; meaning is always carried by text. Clear shapes and strong contrast help us keep it readable.

**Process.** Short discovery chat, 2 concept sketches, one round of changes on the chosen direction, then final art. We will pay fairly and promptly.
