# 22 — Understanding AblePoint Today (from its own live website)

Source: AblePoint's public pages read on 3 Nov 2026 (home, about, services, support team, contact, Dubbo, youth services). These are the owner's published words, **not verified facts**. Everything below needs owner confirmation before it appears on the new site.

## What the current site says AblePoint is

- A registered NDIS provider. Founded "to address service gaps across homelessness, justice and health, particularly for people living with disability who require consistent, person-centred support". The youth page says it was "founded in the heart of Western Sydney".
- Services: supported independent living (group homes of two to five residents), SDA, short term accommodation/respite, core support, support coordination, community access ("Unique Community Services"), and a separate **Ablepoint Youth Service** (out-of-home care, alternative care arrangements, mentoring, transport, 24/7 support).
- Stated specialties: **mental health supports, complex needs clients, and clients with criminal convictions**. The about page also describes participants with trauma, limited support networks and difficulty accessing services.
- Values: equality, respect, honesty, trust, innovation, creativity. Mission: "to live, work, and belong in an inclusive environment".
- Support worker requirements: NDIS Worker Screening check (police and working with children checks), CPR and first aid, relevant experience, COVID-19 infection control training, two professional references.
- Referral promise: "a quick referral response time of less than a week".
- Contact page lists **1/12 Post Office Street, Carlingford NSW 2118** and **Orange NSW 2800**; phone 1300 088 549; hello@ablepointaustralia.com.au.
- The site markets mostly to **Sydney** (titles and copy), while regional pages exist for Newcastle, Dubbo, Central Coast and Orange.

## Opportunities this understanding suggests (feeds `20-seo-growth-contract.md`)

1. **Niche positioning** that competitors rarely own: housing plus support for people with complex needs, mental health support needs, and people transitioning from hospital or justice settings. Support coordinators and discharge planners struggle to place these participants. (Competitor research found one provider with a hospital-discharge page; none of the marketplaces target this.)
2. **Referrer-first content**: pages and a referral flow written for support coordinators, plan managers, hospital discharge and justice liaison staff.
3. **Regional gaps**: no competitor checked has a Gunnedah page; Dubbo and Orange coverage is thin.
4. **Trust**: registration, screening, complaints, accessibility and honest vacancy status shown on every relevant page.

## What was not carried over to the new site

Sydney-centred claims, "best/most trusted/leading/obvious choice" language, the "less than a week" promise, unverified facility claims, the youth/out-of-home-care content, and the Carlingford address, until the owner answers C-1 to C-8.
